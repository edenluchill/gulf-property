1. Definitions

  • "Gross Revenue" means all amounts actually received by or on behalf of the Company from monetising the Platform as a product — i.e. SaaS subscriptions, platform access fees, and per-transaction fees paid by users of the Platform — net of: (i) refunds and chargebacks actually paid, (ii) payment-processor fees (e.g. Stripe), and (iii) any VAT or sales taxes collected on behalf of an authority. It is measured before deduction of any operating cost. For the avoidance of doubt, Gross Revenue does NOT include the Company's or any Founder's own real-estate brokerage commissions earned from property sales or leasing transactions; the License Fee applies only to Platform (SaaS) revenue, never to brokerage commission income.
  • "Net Profit" means the Company's profit after tax determined under IFRS, after deduction of all bona fide operating costs and the License Fee, as shown in accounts audited or reviewed under Section 5.5.
  • "Territory" means the member states of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC).
  • "Platform IP" means all Intellectual Property Rights in and to the Platform, including source code, object code, AI/ML architectures and prompts, algorithms, data schemas, and documentation.
  • "Third-Party Operating Costs" means pass-through costs of third-party services required to run the Platform, including cloud hosting, storage/CDN, AI/model API usage (e.g. Gemini), payment-processor fees, and third-party data feeds.

2. Intellectual Property & License Grant

  • 2.1 Ownership. Party A is and remains the sole and exclusive owner of 100% of the Platform IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers, assigns or co-owns any Platform IP to Party B or the Company. All improvements, modifications and derivatives of the Platform created by or for Party A vest in Party A.
  • 2.2 License Grant. Subject to continued compliance with this Agreement (in particular Sections 3 and 4), Party A grants the Company a non-transferable, non-sublicensable (except to end users in the ordinary course), revocable license to access and use the Platform to operate the business in the Territory during the Term.
  • 2.3 Exclusivity, Conditioned on Performance. The license is exclusive in the Territory for so long as the Company meets the minimum performance milestones in Schedule 2. If the Company fails to meet a milestone for [two] consecutive quarters, Party A may, on written notice, (a) convert the license to non-exclusive, and/or (b) carve out any GCC country in which the Company is not actively operating, and license it to others. Party A shall not license the identical Platform to a direct competitor of the Company in any country where the Company is then meeting its milestones.
  • 2.4 Restrictions. Neither the Company nor any Founder shall, and each shall ensure its personnel do not: (a) copy, decompile, reverse-engineer, or attempt to derive the source code or architecture of the Platform; (b) build a competing platform using knowledge of the Platform's confidential architecture; or (c) remove or obscure any proprietary notice. These restrictions survive termination.
  • 2.5 Background / Pre-Existing IP. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Platform is the pre-existing proprietary technology of Party A (FINDHOMEGO AI TECH INC.), independently designed, developed, owned and operated by Party A prior to and independently of this Agreement (including in connection with Party A's existing Canadian real-estate website). The Company and each Founder acknowledge they have contributed nothing to the creation of the Platform IP, have no ownership, joint-ownership, authorship or "work-made-for-hire" claim to it, and shall not assert any such claim. This Agreement grants only a license (Section 2.2), not any ownership interest, in the Platform IP.
  • 2.6 Technology vs. Brand — Separate Ownership. The Parties expressly distinguish two different sets of rights: (a) the Platform / technology IP (source code, AI architecture, algorithms, infrastructure) is and remains owned by Party A; and (b) the "Pinzos" name, logo, local brand, marketing identity and customer relationships are owned by the Company / Party B. Neither Party acquires ownership of the other's set. On termination each Party keeps its own (Section 9.3): Party A takes the technology (and may redeploy it under a different name), and the Company keeps the "Pinzos" brand and its clients.

3. Confidentiality

  • 3.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other (including, for Party A, the Platform's architecture, code, prompts and algorithms), use it only to perform this Agreement, and protect it with no less than reasonable care. Obligations survive [5] years after termination; trade secrets are protected for as long as they remain secret.
  • 3.2 The Founders acknowledge that the Platform IP constitutes Party A's trade secrets and that unauthorised disclosure would cause irreparable harm for which damages alone are inadequate, entitling Party A to injunctive relief.

4. Data

  • 4.1 Local Data. As between the Parties, the Company owns the raw commercial and user-identifiable data generated by end users of the Company's localized Platform ("Local Data"), and is the data controller responsible for compliance with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and applicable regulations.
  • 4.2 Party A as Processor. To the extent Party A hosts, operates or has technical access to Local Data in providing the Platform, Party A acts as the Company's data processor under the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule 3, which governs Party A's technical/root access (Section 7), cross-border transfer safeguards, and security obligations. Party A's operational access under Section 7 is exercised in that processor capacity and does not give Party A ownership of Local Data.
  • 4.3 Anonymized Data License. The Company grants Party A a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to access and use fully anonymized and aggregated (de-identified) Platform data via API, solely to train, iterate and improve the Platform. Such anonymized data must not permit re-identification of any individual.

5. Equity, Financial Terms & Governance

In consideration of the zero-upfront-cost deployment of the Platform by Party A and the operating capital and effort of Party B, the Parties agree the following combined structure:

5.1 License Fee (top-line)

  • (a) The Company shall pay Party A a continuous license and maintenance fee equal to 25% of Gross Revenue (the "License Fee"). This top-line fee is Party A's primary economic return; in exchange, Party A permanently waives ordinary in-life dividends (Section 5.3), while fully retaining its equity's voting, anti-dilution and exit-proceeds rights.
  • (b) Where technically feasible, the License Fee is settled automatically at the point of transaction via automated payment routing (e.g. Stripe Connect). Amounts not routed automatically are paid monthly within [15] days of month-end, with a statement.
  • (c) Refund/Chargeback Reconciliation. Because the License Fee is computed on Gross Revenue net of refunds and chargebacks, any License Fee previously paid on a subsequently refunded transaction is credited against the next payment (or refunded within 30 days if none).
  • (d) Fixed Rate — Never Diluted. The 25% License Fee rate is fixed for the entire life of this Agreement and is a contractual right of Party A that exists independently of Party A's shareholding. It cannot be reduced, diluted, waived, deferred, set off or renegotiated without Party A's written consent, and it is unaffected by any change in the Company's cap table, ownership, financing round, share issuance, reorganisation or corporate structure. Even if Party A's equity were diluted to any level (or Party A held no shares at all), the 25% License Fee remains payable in full. Any arrangement that routes, books or diverts Platform (SaaS) revenue through an affiliate or third party so as to erode this fee is a material breach (see also anti-bypass, 6.3).

5.2 Equity

  • (a) Allocation. On incorporation, Party A (or its designated affiliate/assignee) shall be issued and registered as holder of 25% of the fully-diluted share capital of the Company, with the Founders holding the remaining 75%. Party A's shares are ordinary shares that dilute pro-rata like any shareholder's on future financings (Party A does not require anti-dilution protection), carrying voting, Reserved-Matter and exit-proceeds rights but no right to ordinary in-life dividends (permanently waived per Section 5.3). Party A's economics are secured through the fixed License Fee (5.1), which is independent of its shareholding — not through its equity percentage or dividends. The Company shall deliver a share certificate and update its register within [30] days of incorporation.
  • (b) Dilution. Party A accepts ordinary dilution: on genuine future financings its 25% may be reduced pro-rata like any other shareholder, with no anti-dilution make-whole. Party A has an optional (non-obligatory) pre-emption right to participate in new issues if it wishes. Party A does not rely on its equity percentage for its economics — the fixed License Fee (5.1) is unaffected by any dilution.
  • (c) Board Seat. Party A is entitled to appoint one director to the Company's board and to receive all information rights (monthly management accounts, annual audited accounts, cap table).
  • (d) Reserved Matters (Veto). None of the following may be done without Party A's prior written consent: (i) issuing, redeeming or reclassifying shares; (ii) altering the constitutional documents in a way affecting Party A's rights; (iii) related-party transactions or above-market compensation to Founders or their affiliates; (iv) amending, assigning or terminating this Agreement or the license; (v) taking on material debt or granting security over the Platform or Local Data; (vi) selling the Company or substantially all its assets; (vii) winding up. Item (iv) — any amendment, assignment or termination of this Agreement or the License, or any change to the License Fee — is a contractual consent right of Party A that survives regardless of Party A's shareholding (it applies even if Party A's equity is diluted or Party A holds no shares). The remaining matters operate through Party A's shareholder rights for so long as it holds shares.
  • (e) What is Locked vs. What is Flexible. The only permanently locked economic term is the 25% License Fee (5.1(d)) — that never changes. Party A's equity is flexible: it is fully-vested on issuance but freely dilutable, and Party A does not depend on it. The Founders' 75% is likewise theirs to hold, dilute or use as they see fit, with no vesting or forfeiture. Party A's up-front consideration (a complete, pre-built platform developed at Party A's own cost of approx. US$[200,000]) is compensated by IP ownership + the fixed License Fee, not by its share count. Party A's protection against a non-performing partner is the milestone-based exclusivity in 2.3 and termination in Section 9.

5.3 Dividends — Permanently Waived

Party A permanently waives its entitlement to ordinary in-life dividends. All Net Profit dividends declared by the Company accrue 100% to the Founders, in consideration of Party A's top-line License Fee (5.1) and the Founders' operational role. This waiver is limited strictly to in-life ordinary dividends. It does not affect, and Party A fully retains at all times: (i) its 25% share of any sale, merger, liquidation or IPO proceeds (i.e. on exit its shares rank pari passu with the Founders'); and (ii) its voting, anti-dilution, pre-emption and Reserved-Matter (veto) rights. In short: no dividends, but on an exit Party A's stake is treated identically to everyone else's.

5.4 Allocation of Costs

To avoid ambiguity: Third-Party Operating Costs are borne by the Company, not deducted from or netted against Party A's License Fee. Party A bears only its own internal cost of maintaining the Platform under Section 6.1. The License Fee is calculated and paid on Gross Revenue regardless of the Company's operating costs.

NOTE: 这条防止"你只拿 25% 却自己贴 Gemini/云费"。云费、AI API、支付手续费全部算 Company 的运营成本,你的 25% 从毛收入顶部走,不受影响。

5.5 Books, Audit & Taxes

  • The Company keeps proper books under IFRS. Party A may, once per year at its cost, audit records relevant to Gross Revenue and the License Fee; an underpayment above [5%] shifts the audit cost to the Company.
  • Each Party bears its own taxes. The Parties shall structure the License Fee and dividends taking account of UAE corporate tax, any withholding tax, and the Canada–UAE position, and cooperate on tax-residency and treaty documentation.

5.6 Party A's Exit — Bundled Sale & Buy-Out

Party A's shares (5.2) and its License Fee rights (5.1) together constitute the "Party A Interest". Party A's liquidity is realised as follows:

  • (a) Bundled Transfer. Party A may transfer the Party A Interest — the 25% equity and the 25% License Fee stream — together as a single package to a third party. (Party A may also transfer either component separately, but the Company's consent, not unreasonably withheld, is required to separate them.)
  • (b) Company / Founder Right of First Refusal. Before any third-party sale, Party A shall offer the Party A Interest to the Founders (or the Company) on the same price and terms; they have [30] days to accept. If they decline or do not complete, Party A may sell to the third party within [90] days on terms no more favourable to the buyer, who then assumes Party A's rights and obligations.
  • (c) Buy-Out / Put Option (defined exit price). At any time after [3] years from first commercial launch, Party A may, by written notice, require the Founders/Company to purchase the entire Party A Interest (a "put"), and the Company may elect to purchase it (a "call"), at a price equal to: [X]× the trailing-12-month License Fee (valuing the royalty stream) plus the fair-market value of the 25% equity (or the price implied by the most recent funding round), payable within [90] days or on agreed instalments. On completion, the License terminates and continuity of the Platform is addressed under Section 9.3 / any agreed handover or escrow release.
NOTE: 这条把你的退出讲死了 —— 你最值钱的是那条 25% 永久授权费(可整体打包卖的现金流资产),最可能的买家就是对方自己(想一次性买断别再永远付你)。(c) 给你一个"按公式强制卖给对方"的 put option,等于给你一条兜底套现出路,不怕股票卖不掉。

6. Roles & Obligations

  • 6.1 Party A. Provide, host/operate and maintain the Platform; deliver updates and security patching; and protect the architecture from external vulnerabilities. Party A shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the Platform available, targeting [99.0%] monthly uptime. Uptime is measured monthly and excludes: (i) scheduled maintenance notified at least [24] hours in advance; (ii) outages or degradation of third-party services (cloud host, CDN, DNS, payment or AI providers); (iii) force majeure; and (iv) issues caused by the Company, its users or its configurations. Failure to meet the uptime target is not a material breach and gives no right to terminate or claim damages; the Company's sole and exclusive remedy is a pro-rata service credit against the License Fee for the affected period. Party A does not warrant uninterrupted or error-free operation.
  • 6.2 Party B / Company. Complete UAE incorporation and obtain and maintain all real-estate brokerage licenses (e.g. RERA) and regulatory approvals; fund and execute all localized marketing, sales, personnel and go-to-market; fund Third-Party Operating Costs; comply with the anti-bypass rule in 6.3; and ensure end-user terms disclaim reliance on AI outputs (Section 8.3). The Founders shall commit and actually deploy at least US$[amount] of operating/marketing capital within the first [12] months (Schedule 2); failure is a Schedule 2 milestone default affecting exclusivity (2.3). (This protects Party A's royalty base — the Platform only earns if the business is actually funded — without touching the Founders' equity.)
  • 6.3 Anti-Bypass. All platform access fees and subscriptions must be processed through the authorised digital payment gateway. Collecting off-gateway payments (cash, cheque, external transfer) in exchange for Platform access to avoid the License Fee is a material breach.

7. Technical Security & Suspension (Conditional)

  • 7.1 Root Access. As operator of the Platform infrastructure, Party A retains administrative/root access to the systems and DNS necessary to run, secure and protect the Platform, exercised as data processor under Schedule 3. Party A shall not use such access to appropriate Local Data or to harm the Company except to exercise its rights under 7.2.
  • 7.2 Suspension for Cause (replaces "Kill Switch"). Party A may suspend the license/API access only where: (a) the Company fails to pay the License Fee when due, or breaches the anti-bypass rule (6.3), or breaches Sections 2.4/3; and (b) Party A has given written notice and the breach remains uncured after a cure period of [10] business days (or [3] business days for anti-bypass/non-payment). Suspension is proportionate and lifted on cure. Only an uncured, repeated or willful material breach permits termination under Section 9.

  • 8.1 Party A Warranty. Party A warrants it owns or is licensed to provide the Platform and that, to its knowledge, the core code it authored does not infringe third-party IP. This excludes third-party components, APIs and data feeds, which are provided subject to their own terms.
  • 8.2 Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality/IP (Sections 2–3) and each Party's indemnity, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, and each Party's aggregate liability is capped at the total License Fees paid in the [12] months before the claim. The Platform is provided on an "as-is" basis beyond the Section 6.1 service standard.
  • 8.3 AI / No Advice Disclaimer. Outputs of the Platform (including ROI projections, area analyses and investment estimates) are informational only and are not financial, investment, legal or valuation advice. The Company shall surface this disclaimer to end users and is responsible for how outputs are presented and marketed locally.
  • 8.4 Indemnities. The Company/Founders indemnify Party A against claims arising from the local operation, marketing, brokerage conduct, regulatory non-compliance, or presentation of Platform outputs to end users. Party A indemnifies the Company against third-party claims that the core Platform code authored by Party A infringes their IP, subject to the cap in 8.2 (except the cap does not apply to Party A's IP indemnity up to [amount/multiple]).

9. Term, Termination & Exit

  • 9.1 Term. Initial term of [5] years from the Effective Date, renewing automatically for successive [2]-year terms unless either Party gives [6] months' notice.
  • 9.2 Termination. Either Party may terminate for the other's uncured material breach (per the cure mechanism in 7.2), insolvency, or a prohibited change of control (10.2).
  • 9.3 Effect of Termination / Clean Exit. On termination or mutual dissolution: (a) the license ends and the Company ceases using the Platform; (b) the Company retains the "Pinzos" local brand and its client relationships, subject to (d); (c) Party A retains all Platform IP and may redeploy globally, including in the Territory — except that, for [12] months after termination, Party A shall not license the identical Platform to a direct competitor of the Company within [Dubai / the Emirate(s) where the Company was actively operating] (this post-term non-compete does not apply where termination was caused by the Company's uncured material breach); (d) Party A is entitled to a one-time export of the fully anonymized dataset (per 4.3) and the Parties shall run an orderly wind-down of paying customers; (e) Sections 2.1, 2.4, 3, 8 and 10 survive.
  • (f) Platform Buy-Out at Exit (optional — Party A's discretion). At or after termination, the Company/Founders may elect to acquire a perpetual, non-exclusive license to, and a deployable frozen copy of (via escrow release), the then-current Platform, for use solely within [Dubai / the Company's operating market] and expressly not to compete with Party A in any other market, in consideration of a buy-out payment agreed by the Parties or determined per the Section 5.6(c) formula. Party A is under no obligation to accept any price it considers, in its sole discretion, inadequate, and Party A retains ownership of the underlying Platform IP (this is a license of a copy, not an assignment). Absent completion of such a purchase, Section 9.3(c) applies and Party A retains the Platform exclusively.
